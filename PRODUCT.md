# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Users

Local customers looking for an appetizing pizza-and-kitchen option, especially people deciding what to order for delivery or pickup. Their immediate job is to understand the menu and start an order through WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Cheese Muelo is a restaurant landing page that presents its menu, featured items, promotions, and brand personality, then routes a prospective customer into a WhatsApp order conversation.

## Positioning

The experience combines artisanal pizza as the lead offering with complementary kitchen items, expressed through the distinctive Cheese Muelo mascot and a playful, high-flavor voice.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive primarily on mobile, review dishes and promotional combinations, and use a WhatsApp link rather than an on-site payment flow. The in-page pizza builder is a guided order request, not a transactional checkout.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a static, responsive landing page built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Images must be referenced through stable local paths so that adding final files does not require code changes.
- Menu items, prices, hours, location, delivery conditions, and testimonials are demonstration content until the client supplies approved values.
- The implementation must use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and optimized local assets.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Cheese Muelo.
- The supplied mascot logo is a required brand asset; its dark character, amber cheese tones, and lime-green eyes are recognizable traits to preserve.
- The supplied design reference establishes a bold, playful, artisanal fast-casual personality with charcoal, cheddar amber, basil green, and warm parchment as committed visual inputs.

## Product Principles

- Make the food and the ordering action immediately understandable.
- Keep the brand bold and playful without reducing readability or mobile usability.
- Treat every commercial detail as editable content, not hard-coded product truth.
- Let final local assets replace placeholders without structural code changes.
